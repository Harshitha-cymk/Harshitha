About Me

Bridging Computational Mathematics with Machine Intelligence

I am R Harshitha, currently pursuing my undergraduate studies at Jain (Deemed-to-be University). My academic journey is fueled by a passion for understanding the fundamental mathematical structures that govern modern artificial intelligence.

While standard deep learning handles grid and sequential data like images and text, real-world problems—such as social graphs, molecular compounds, and knowledge networks—are modeled as complex graphs. By combining rigorous Discrete Mathematics with Graph Neural Networks, I focus on building models that can effectively reason over relational data.
